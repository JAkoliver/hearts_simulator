How Hearts works

Goal: take the FEWEST points. Hearts are 1 point each, the Q♠ is 13. A match plays deals until someone reaches 100 - lowest total wins.

Each deal: pass 3 cards (direction cycles left / right / across / none), then the 2♣ leads. You must follow suit when you can; hearts can't LEAD a trick until one has been played (the ♥ badge lights when they're broken).

Shooting the moon: take ALL 26 points in a deal and the other three players get 26 each instead.

Timers: at a hosted table with a turn timer, running out auto-plays your lowest card - deliberately weak, so stalling never helps.

After a match: Analyze match opens the full review - every play judged by the AI, with deep search on your own hardware.
