Match-aware

Trained on whole games to 100, not isolated deals. Passes and plays are valued in match-win chance: it will eat points today to win the match tomorrow.

Runs on your device

The deep search runs in your browser: GPU when available, multi-core CPU otherwise. Search values are shared with your match's viewers; nothing personal ever leaves your device.
