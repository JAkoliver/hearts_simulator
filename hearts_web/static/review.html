Match review

How this review works

Two different opinions, never mixed

Perilune's view (right panel) shows the raw network's habit: how often it would play each card. Big percentages mean strong instinct, not that a move is right.

Deep search (the cyan bars) shows value: each move is actually played out to the end of the deal, many times, with the hidden cards guessed the way the acting seat would have to guess them. This is the opinion worth trusting - and it runs on your own device.

The numbers on a search bar

7.4 pts - average penalty points that seat takes this deal if it plays that card.
−2.0% match - match-win chance given up versus the best move. Match win, not deal win: winning a deal early in a long match is worth only a few points of match equity.
Equivalent cards (a connected run) are pooled into one entry - the net has no real preference between them.

Verdicts

cost −x% - the search is confident this play gave up real match-win chance. no cost - your card matched the best move's value; any raw-policy complaint was habit. ? try K=N - the gap might be real but is inside the noise at this depth; deepen the position to settle it.

K, and the two buttons

K is how many imagined deals each move is played out over. Higher K = sharper numbers, slower. Deep analysis sweeps the whole match at the K you pick; K=N here deepens just the position you are looking at (16 → 32 → 64 → 128 → 256). Results are cached per position, so raising K only re-analyzes what is below it.

Par, cards-only, and decision costs

Par (deal start) is what a deal's cards cost with Perilune playing all four hands - a luck-inclusive reference, not a skill score. Cards-only match replays the whole match with Perilune in every seat over the same dealt hands: above 25% means the cards favored that seat. Decision costs adds up each seat's confident mistakes - that is the skill side. Cumulative costs can exceed 100% because chances lost can be won back when others err.

Getting around

Drag or click the strip to scrub; ←/→ step, ↑/↓ jump deals, D next deal, T next trick. Click any entry in the left panel to jump to it - the current one stays highlighted.

Every judgment is made from what the acting seat could see, even though the review shows all hands. Perilune is strong but not perfect - where it has blind spots (moon defense especially), its opinions inherit them.
