Perilune.

per·i·lune - the lowest point of an orbit around the Moon.

Hearts is a game where the lowest score wins and the boldest play is shooting the moon. A perilune is the point of a lunar orbit closest to the Moon - the lowest point, at the moon. One word, both ideas. That's the name of the AI you're playing against.

What this is

Perilune is a neural network trained to play Hearts as a match - games to 100, lowest total wins - rather than deal by deal. It learned by playing millions of games against itself, guided by search and a learned sense of match-winning equity. This site is its public table: one human seat (or up to four, at a hosted table) against the current strongest network.

What gets recorded

Matches are recorded anonymously to improve the AI: the cards played, timing, and outcomes of each deal, tied to a random identifier stored in your browser. No accounts, no emails, no personal information. Human games are especially valuable because people find weaknesses self-play never does.

The project

Perilune is an ongoing experiment in training a card-game AI end to end - engine, search, training loops, and measurement discipline. An open-source release of the code, models, and methodology is planned.
