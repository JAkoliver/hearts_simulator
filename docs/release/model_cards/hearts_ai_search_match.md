trace + equity model at the deployment K schedule

## Measured strength
- **N=8000 validation:** with equity leaf scoring, wins **48.91% vs
  44.47%** against the frozen match-blind reference (+4.44 win-points,
  SE 0.68; McNemar one-sided p ~ 5e-11; all 8 shards positive; ledger
  2026-07-27; data equity_data/validation_v1/).
- Placement structure: converts P2 into both P1 and P4 (P1 3913 v
  3558, P4 1780 v 969); mean placement WORSE (2.098 v 1.980) — it
  trades expected placement for win probability, the objective.
- Exploratory: effect concentrates in short matches (+13.3 win-pts
  short / +0.9 mid / -5.9 long tercile; endogenous split, labeled
  exploratory; ledger 2026-07-27).
- Opponent-field caveat: measured vs the frozen reference under the
  registered configuration only.

## Known weaknesses (stated honestly)
- Search-based moon defense is substantially better than the raw net's
  (1.208 vs 2.417 moons/match conceded, CRN-paired, p<1e-5; ledger
  2026-08-08) but the long-match tercile above suggests the win-equity
  objective can sacrifice placement in long grinds.
- Wall-clock: ~26 s per paired match at the deployment schedule on the
  dev 4090 (rules #15 cost note); it is not a real-time-cheap player.

## Intended use
Teacher/expert for data generation; strongest-play configuration for
evaluation. Not a gate baseline (that is the raw champion) and not the
frozen validation reference (that is the matchblind_20260724 artifact).
