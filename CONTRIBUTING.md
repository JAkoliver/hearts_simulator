# Contributing

Perilune is released as an **open research record**, not as a
maintained community project.

What that means in practice:

- **Fork freely.** MIT license: clone it, modify your copy, build on
  it, ship things with it. That is the point of releasing it.
- **Issues** are welcome for: reproduction problems (a build step that
  fails, a manifest hash that does not match, a documented number you
  cannot trace to its citation), factual errors in the docs, and
  security reports for the live site.
- **Pull requests are generally not merged.** The repository is the
  project's measurement record; its history and artifacts are cited by
  hash throughout the docs, and the measurement discipline
  (docs/experiment_rules.md) does not admit drive-by changes to
  instruments, gates, or data. Small documentation fixes may be
  accepted; anything touching engine, models, gates, or recorded
  results will not be.
- **No support commitment.** Questions may be answered as time allows;
  the docs (docs/release/INDEX.md reading order) are the intended
  interface.

If you reproduce a result, or fail to, that is genuinely interesting -
open an issue with your numbers and environment.

The live site and the GPU rentals behind these experiments are funded
by players: [play.perilune.ai/support](https://play.perilune.ai/support).
